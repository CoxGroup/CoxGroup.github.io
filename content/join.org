* PhD positions

Two fully funded 4-year PhD positions are available (UK fees),
funded by the Royal Society.

Project details and further information will be made available in
early Autumn. In the meantime, feel free to [[mailto:stephen.j.cox@durham.ac.uk][get in touch]] to make
informal enquiries.

* Postdoctoral positions and fellowships

Postdoctoral positions will be advertised when funding becomes
available.

We're also very happy to support applications for independent
fellowships, such as [[https://royalcommission1851.org/fellowships/research-fellowships][1851 Research Fellowships]] or [[https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/][Marie
Skłodowska-Curie Fellowships]]. If you're considering applying, [[mailto:stephen.j.cox@durham.ac.uk][get in
touch]] to discuss a project.
